# T3 effects and the Mastering Assistant

Phase 11, workstream E ([docs/12-phase-11.md](../12-phase-11.md)). In `engine/src/Anode.Dsp`: Saturation (an
`AudioEffect`), the Down Mixer and Binaural Post-Processing as format-changing inserts (`IFormatEffect`), the
Compressor's and Limiter's channel link for surround strips, and the Mastering Assistant (`Mastering/`). All are
registered: `BuiltinEffects` (saturation, the assistant, held to `EffectContractTests`' theories) and
`BuiltinFormatEffects` (the two inserts, held to `T3ContractTests`).

## The effects

| Id | Name | Category | Algorithm | Latency |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| `anode.saturation` | Saturation | Distortion | Drive 0 … 30 dB, the model's emphasis, its shaper at 4× (`Oversampler4x`), de-emphasis, DC blocker (8 Hz), tone (a tilt: shelves at 800 Hz, ±6 dB each way), make-up so a −12 dBFS signal keeps its level at any drive, output, mix. Tube: `tanh(x + 0.3) − tanh(0.3)` (even and odd). Tape: `x/√(1 + x²)` between +6 dB pre-emphasis above 3 kHz and its inverse. Transformer: +6 dB below 120 Hz into a cubic core `1.5x − 0.5x³` (±1, a slight offset), then given back. Modern: `x/(1 + x⁴)^¼`. | 0 |
| `anode.down-mixer` | Down Mixer | Utility | Phase 10's BS.775 `Downmixer` matrix from the strip's layout to `format` (when that has fewer channels; else it passes the input). The LFE's route into the front pair is linear in its gain, so `Prepare` builds the matrix without the LFE and the LFE's column at unit gain; `lfe` (dB, −144 dropped) scales that column, ramped on a 32-sample grid. | 0 |
| `anode.binaural` | Binaural Post-Processing | Utility | Phase 10's `BinauralRenderer` (each speaker a virtual speaker at its nominal position, partitioned HRTF convolution, the LFE to both ears) with the set from `UseHrtfs` (the Server's, in `EngineInsert.file`); without one it down-mixes to stereo (BS.775) so the strip is never silent. ### Channel link (Compressor, Limiter)

**Not in the contracts; added here** (docs/12-phase-11.md, "Not contracted here"). Both now end with a parameter
**`link`** ("Channel Link"), a choice of **`All`** (0, the default: every channel's level drives one gain, as Phase 10's
`MultichannelPolicy.Linked` does) and **`By Speaker Group`** (1: each speaker group its own gain, from its own
channels). `Anode.Dsp.Spatial.ChannelLink` holds the values (`All`, `ByGroup`, `Names`) and the grouping:
`GroupOf(layout, channel)` → `SpeakerGroup` (`Front`: the ear layer's |azimuth| < 90°, L R C and the wides; `Lfe`;
`Surround`: the ear layer's |azimuth| ≥ 90°; `Height`: the upper layer) and `Groups(layout, link)` (per channel the
index of the gain it shares: all 0 for `All`, the group's number for `ByGroup`; 7.1.4 by group is
`0 0 0 1 2 2 2 2 3 3 3 3`). A stereo strip ignores it (tested bit-identical). The engine's linked policy
(`MultiInsert`) runs one instance, key and gain per speaker group of the strip's layout from `Compressor.Link`/`Limiter.Link`;
the groups' instances exist on every surround strip, so the link can move at any time.

## Mastering Assistant

**Analysis** (`MasteringAnalyzer.Analyse(left, right, rate)`, on an offline render of the master before it):
- Loudness by Phase 4's `LoudnessMeter`: integrated (BS.1770-4 gated), the loudest short-term value (after 3 s, or of
  the whole render when shorter), loudness range by EBU Tech 3342 (short-term values every 100 ms, gated at −70 LUFS
  and 20 LU below their mean, P95 − P10).
- True peak by `TruePeak`'s 4× interpolation (the limiters' detector); crest factor = true peak over the RMS of both
  channels; width = side energy / mid energy, clamped to 0 … 1.
- Spectrum: the mid's power averaged over 16,384-sample Hann frames (half overlapping, at 48 kHz), summed per
  `MasteringBands` band (centre × 2^±1/6), dB, smoothed 1-2-1, less the bands' mean.

**Curves** (`MasteringAnalyzer.Curve(character)`, relative to their mean): a typical master's third-octave balance —
flat 40 … 150 Hz, −2.5 dB/octave to 2 kHz, −3.5 dB/octave above, a further −4 dB/octave above 12 kHz, −6 dB/octave
below 40 Hz. Valve adds +1.5 dB around 200 Hz and −1.5 dB above 6 kHz; Punch +2 dB around 70 Hz and +1 dB around
3 kHz; Transparent is Clean's (applied at half weight).

**Design** (`MasteringChainDesigner.Design(measurement, character)` → `MasteringChainSettings`, the IPC's
`EngineMasteringChain`): EQ = curve − spectrum, smoothed 1-2-1 twice, within ±4 dB (±2 dB at 25, 31.5 Hz and 20 kHz),
Transparent ×½, nothing for a silent measurement; compressor threshold −13.3 dBFS (4 dB over the RMS of the working
level, below), ratio 1.5 … 2 with the loudness range (4 … 12 LU), +0.3 for Punch, half way to 1 for Transparent;
`LoudnessLufs` the integrated loudness; `ChainGainDb` the K-weighted loudness change of the EQ (band powers × the
K-weighting × the gains) plus an estimate of the compressor's reduction.

**The chain** (`MasteringAssistant`, per sample, `SetChain` from the control thread, swapped in at the next grid
point):
1. The input normalised to the working level, **−18 LUFS** (`WorkingLufs`), from `LoudnessLufs`, so the EQ, the
   compressor and the colour always work at one level.
2. EQ: 30 bells (SVF, Q 4.318 — a third of an octave) at the band centres; their gains are solved through the bells'
   overlap (the inverse of the 30 × 30 matrix of each bell's dB response at every centre, built at `Prepare`), so the
   response at each centre is the designer's gain × the `eq` strength.
3. Compressor: stereo-linked, 10 ms RMS detector (summed mean square), 6 dB knee, attack 10 ms (Punch 30 ms), release
   150 ms, ratio moved toward 1 by `dynamics`.
4. Colour: Valve a biased tube curve (`tanh(1.5x + 0.25)`, unity slope at rest) at 4× (even harmonics); Punch a fast
   (1 / 20 ms) over slow (15 / 120 ms) envelope lifting transients up to +3.6 dB; Clean and Transparent none. A change
   of character ramps the colour in or out over a control interval.
5. Width: the side × `width` (0 … 2).
6. Band limit: 4th-order Butterworth at 19.5 kHz (≤ 0.42 of the rate) — the true-peak detector's interpolator, as a
   BS.1770 meter's, is specified to 20 kHz; without it near-Nyquist content read 0.4 dB over the ceiling.
7. The limiter's input gain, then `LookaheadLimiter` (adaptive, true peak, 2 ms lookahead, 80 ms release) under
   `ceilingDbtp` − 0.3 dB.

**Reaching the target.** The total gain before the limiter is `targetLufs − loudnessLufs − chainGain`, the IPC's
formula. The chain gain starts at the designer's estimate (× the `eq` strength) and is then **measured as it plays**:
the BS.1770 integrated loudness of the limiter's output at unit input gain over that of the normalised input since the
last reset (both gated as BS.1770 gates; time counted where the input's momentary loudness is within 20 LU of the
working level), blended with the estimate as if that had half a second of weight; the gain follows with a 0.25 s time
constant. So the limiter's own loss, the width, the colour and whatever the estimate missed are made up within the
first second, and a bounce's integrated loudness lands on the target (the golden path's mix: −13.98 to −14.09
LUFS). It is a ratio of two loudnesses, not a level: a quiet passage is not lifted. (Open loop, the estimate
alone missed by up to 1 LU on bright material.) The target is of what the analysis measured: a section more than 10 LU
under the rest, gated out of the analysis, comes back above the gate once the limiter has narrowed the loud part, and
the whole lands under the target (a 16-bar song whose last 8 bars were drums alone: −15.8 LUFS).

Without a chain it is the limiter alone at unit gain (no band limit: an impulse comes out at exactly the reported
latency, unchanged). The level-matched bypass plays the input, delayed by the latency, at `targetLufs − loudnessLufs`,
crossfaded over a control interval. Meters (for A's `MasteringEntry` and `/ws mastering`): `MomentaryLufs`,
`ShortTermLufs`, `IntegratedLufs` (since the reset), `TruePeakDbtp` (since the reset), and `IMasteringChain`'s
`CompressorReductionDb` and `LimiterReductionDb` (deepest over the last buffer); the `Reading` is the limiter's.
`TotalGainDb` is the gain before the limiter now.

## Measured

48 kHz; tests in `engine/tests/Anode.Dsp.Tests`; `T3DemoTests.Measure_t3` (`ANODE_DSP_DEMO=1`) writes the tables.

**Saturation**, THD (harmonics 2 … 10) of a 1 kHz sine at −12 dBFS; the test requires a monotone rise over 10, 40,
80 % for every model:

| Model | Drive 10 % | 40 % | 80 % | 100 % |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Tube | −26.1 | −18.6 | −10.3 | −8.3 |
| Tape | −39.5 | −24.6 | −14.3 | −12.4 |
| Transformer | −34.8 | −18.3 | −8.4 | −7.6 |
| Modern | −58.2 | −25.5 | −9.8 | −8.1 |

At 50 % the tube's 2nd harmonic is > 20 dB over the tape's; tape and modern are odd (H3 > H2 + 20 dB); the level stays
within ±1.5 dB of the input. Mix 50 % is exactly half dry and half wet; Mix 0 is the dry signal (no latency).

**Down Mixer**: every coefficient of 5.1 → 2.0 (BS.775: L' = L + 0.7071 C + 0.7071 Ls, LFE dropped; with `lfe` 0 dB
it joins at −3 dB, at −10 dB 10 dB lower), 7.1.4 → 2.0 (docs/spatial/README.md's matrix) and 7.1.4 → 5.1 (Phase 10's)
within 0.1 dB of the reference (tested; the matrix is Phase 10's, so it is exact).

**Binaural Post-Processing**, 7.1.4, an impulse in M+090 (90° left): ILD 17.1 dB, ITD 0.71 ms (tests require ≥ 6 dB
and 0.5 … 0.8 ms); the mirror ≤ −6 dB; the centre within ±1.5 dB.

**Mastering Assistant**, 12 s of material (`Support/Masters`), output integrated loudness (LUFS) / true peak (dBTP,
a 64-tap Blackman-Harris 4× interpolator, finer than the limiter's); the tests require −14 ± 0.5 LUFS and ≤ −1 dBTP
for every material and character:

| Material | Input | Clean | Valve | Punch | Transparent |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Quiet | −30.0 LUFS, −17.6 dBTP | −13.92 / −2.12 | −13.94 / −2.05 | −13.73 / −1.61 | −13.98 / −2.32 |
| Loud | −8.0 LUFS, −0.0 dBTP | −14.02 / −3.37 | −14.05 / −3.11 | −13.87 / −2.23 | −14.01 / −4.97 |
| Bass-heavy | −16.0 LUFS, −3.8 dBTP | −14.07 / −2.20 | −14.11 / −2.45 | −14.01 / −1.68 | −14.06 / −2.58 |
| Bright | −20.0 LUFS, −4.2 dBTP | −14.28 / −1.84 | −14.33 / −2.07 | −14.16 / −1.56 | −14.29 / −1.80 |

Also tested: −9, −18 (ceiling −2) and −23 LUFS targets on the loud material; the assistant's own integrated meter
within 0.1 LU of the measured output; Valve's even harmonics on a 1 kHz tone ≥ 6 dB over Clean's (Clean's are at the
numerical floor); the level-matched bypass within 1 LU of the processed loudness and exactly the delayed input at
`target − loudness`; width 0 is mono, 2 at least doubles the side; `eq` 0 and `dynamics` 0 still reach the target.

## Tests

- `T3ContractTests`: `EffectContractTests`' theories over Saturation and the assistant (twice: without and with a
  chain): allocation-free under a parameter sweep, finite at extremes, bit-exact buffer-size invariance, reset clears
  history, state round trip, unprepared pass-through, Saturation's dry at Mix 0, the unanalysed assistant's latency.
  The format inserts (Down Mixer on 5.1 and 7.1.4, Binaural with and without HRTFs): output formats, no allocation,
  buffer-size invariance, reset and state.
- `T3EffectTests`: THD by drive per model; the models' harmonics and level; tone, output and mix; the down-mix
  coefficients; binaural ILD/ITD; the fallback; the channel link.
- `MasteringTests`: the analysis on known signals (a sine's loudness, peak, crest, width; an inter-sample over;
  silence); curves and design; the 16 material × character cases; other targets; Valve against Clean; matched bypass;
  strengths, width and no chain.
- `T3GoldenRenderTests`: `Golden/anode.saturation*.wav` (four models), `anode.mastering-assistant.wav` (Valve chain on
  `Fx.Music`), `anode.down-mixer.wav` and `anode.binaural.wav` (7.1.4 to stereo).

## For the other workstreams

- **A (engine):** `IMasteringChain` (A's interface, implemented as declared) — pass `EngineInsert.mastering` as
  `MasteringChainSettings` to `SetChain` (it copies the EQ gains; a wrong count throws `ArgumentException`). The
  assistant's latency is constant once prepared (2 ms lookahead + 16). `BinauralInsert.UseHrtfs(HrtfSet)` before
  `Prepare`. The format inserts' `Prepare(input, output, …)` takes the output layout `OutputFormat(input)` gives;
  `DownMixerInsert.Gain(output, input)` is the matrix now. Channel link: `Compressor.Link`, `Limiter.Link` and
  `ChannelLink.Groups`.
- **B (server):** compile `EngineInsert.mastering` with `MasteringChainDesigner.Design(measurement, character)` from
  the stored analysis and the insert's current `character` (redesign when the character changes: the EQ is the
  character's curve, Transparent's at half weight). `MasteringMeasurement` maps one to one onto the contract's
  `MasteringAnalysis` (with `curveDb` = `MasteringAnalyzer.Curve(character)`).
