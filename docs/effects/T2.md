# T2 effects and Remix FX

Phase 6, workstream D ([07-phase-6.md](../07-phase-6.md), "D — T2 effects and Remix FX"). The fifteen effects below are
built-ins in `engine/src/Anode.Dsp/Effects`, registered in `BuiltinEffects` after the T1 suite, against `IAudioEffect`
and the Phase 6 transport context. Their tests are in `engine/tests/Anode.Dsp.Tests` (`T2EffectTests`,
`SpectralTests`, and the contract and golden-render theories, which run over every built-in).

## The effects

| Id | Name | Category | Algorithm | Latency | Transport |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---|
| `anode.space` | Space | Reverb | Convolution: uniformly partitioned FFT (overlap-save, frequency-domain delay line) behind a direct-form head of one partition; partition 64 … 1,024 by √(4·taps); a generated 2.5 s hall or a WAV impulse response (resampled, energy-normalised); pre-delay, length (partition gains with a fade), low/high cut on the wet path, mix | 0 | – |
| `anode.linear-eq` | Linear Phase EQ | EQ | The Channel EQ's eight bands (same ids) as one FIR: the product of the RBJ magnitudes on 2,049 bins, zero phase, inverse FFT, centred and Blackman-windowed (4,096 taps at 48 kHz), run by the partitioned convolver; redesigned at most every 128 samples while a parameter moves | 2,048 at 48 kHz (`DesignSize / 2`) | – |
| `anode.multipressor` | Multipressor | Dynamics | Four bands by three LR4 crossovers (SVF), lower bands all-pass-compensated so the bands sum flat; per band a linked peak detector, compression above the threshold (6 dB knee), downward expansion below the expander threshold within a reduction range, attack/release, make-up; output, mix | 0 | – |
| `anode.enveloper` | Enveloper | Dynamics | Transient designer: a 10 ms mean-square level, an attack pair (0.5 ms vs Attack Time rise) and a release pair (Release Time vs 20 ms fall) compared in dB; gain = Attack Gain × transient gap + Release Gain × tail gap (±12 dB), above a threshold; lookahead delays the audio | the lookahead (0 … 20 ms) | – |
| `anode.exciter` | Exciter | Specialized | 24 dB/oct high-pass at the frequency, a tanh (odd) or asymmetric (even and odd) shaper scaled back by its drive, a 12 dB/oct high-pass after, added at Harmonics | 0 | – |
| `anode.subbass` | SubBass | Specialized | Band-pass on the mono sum, zero crossings with hysteresis toggle a divide-by-2 (and -4) flip-flop, scaled by the band's envelope and low-passed (24 dB/oct) into a near-sine, added at Level | 0 | – |
| `anode.delay-designer` | Delay Designer | Delay | 26 taps (A … Z) on one 10 s line of the mono sum: per tap time (cubic, gliding), level, constant-power pan, low/high cut, mute; tap count; sync snaps tap times to a grid note value; feedback from a chosen tap; mix | 0 | tempo (grid) |
| `anode.pitch-shifter` | Pitch Shifter | Pitch | Two read heads on a delay line at the shifted speed, sin² crossfades over a window; a head restarts half a window from the other at the lag of best normalised cross-correlation (coarse then fine), so the heads are in phase while they crossfade; ±24 semitones and cents; mix | 0 | – |
| `anode.pitch-correction` | Pitch Correction | Pitch | YIN every 256 samples by FFT (80 … 1,000 Hz, trough 0.12, voicing 0.25, −70 dBFS gate); target the nearest note of the scale in the key plus detune, glided by Response; TD-PSOLA with fractional analysis and synthesis marks and interpolated reads, normalised by the windows' sum; mix of the equally delayed dry | 1,202 at 48 kHz (2·⌈rate/80⌉ + 2, 25 ms) | – |
| `anode.ensemble` | Ensemble | Modulation | Up to 8 voices of the mono sum through modulated delays: a sine per voice (rates spread ±30 %) plus a random wander; panned across the field, summed at 1/√voices; mix | 0 | – |
| `anode.rotor-cabinet` | Rotor Cabinet | Modulation | Leslie: LR4 split at 800 Hz; horn and drum rotors with slow/fast/brake rates reached exponentially (drum 3× slower); two mics: horn Doppler (modulated delay) and amplitude, drum amplitude; horn/drum balance, mic distance and angle; mix | 0 | – |
| `anode.phat-fx` | Phat FX | Multi Effects | Low shelf boost → soft/hard/fuzz distortion → resonant 24 dB low-pass moved by an LFO synced to the song position and an envelope follower → squash compressor → output; mix | 0 | tempo and position (LFO) |
| `anode.step-fx` | Step FX | Multi Effects | Three 16-step sequencers (cutoff of a 12 dB multimode filter between two limits, volume, pan), each step at the song position ÷ the rate, smoothing between steps; volume and pan per sample; mix | 0 | tempo and position |
| `anode.beat-breaker` | Beat Breaker | Multi Effects | A pattern of 16 slices over 1/2/4/8 beats on the song position; each slice plays a source slice (this pass if already heard, else the previous), reversed or muted, from a 12 s history; fades beside rearranged slices; mix | 0 | tempo and position |
| `anode.remix-fx` | Remix FX | Multi Effects | Two XY pads, every control a parameter: pad 1 filter (DJ low/high-pass), wobble (synced LFO filter), orbit (synced flanger); pad 2 repeater (captures the grid division begun at the touch), reverse, scratch, gater, downsampler, tape stop; each pad fades in and out over 5 ms; transparent while untouched | 0 | tempo and position |

Shared helpers, in `engine/src/Anode.Dsp/Spectral`: `RealFft` (a single-precision real FFT on split arrays, twiddle and
bit-reversal tables, allocation-free) and `PartitionedConvolver` (the zero-latency convolver Space and the Linear Phase
EQ run). `Metering.SplitFft` stays as it was.

### Every built-in

- **Macro hints.** Every built-in effect, T1 included, declares its macro hints (`EffectParameters.Describe(…, macros)`,
  orders 0, 1, 2 … in the order listed): the parameters Smart Controls map first. A test checks that each effect has some
  and that their orders run 0 … n − 1.
- **Transport.** `AudioEffect` implements `SetTransport`: it keeps the context, advances it by itself if the engine
  does not renew it, and gives the effects `HostTempo(fallback)`, `Playing`, `QuarterAt(frame)` and
  `QuartersPerSample`. While the transport does not play (or before any engine passes one), synced effects run on a
  free clock at the tempo, counted on the control grid from `Reset`, so their output still does not depend on the buffer
  size. A position within `GridEpsilon` (10⁻⁹ quarter notes) of a grid line counts as on it, so a step whose boundary is
  an exact sample starts on that sample.
- **T1 tempo.** The Stereo Delay, Tape Delay, Tremolo and AutoFilter take their tempo from the transport when the engine
  passes one (`HostTempo(Value(Tempo))`) and from their `tempo` parameter otherwise, as before. Without a transport their
  output is unchanged (their golden renders still null).

## Measured

On an Apple M1 Max (10 cores), .NET 10.0.14, Release, 48 kHz.

### Accuracy

| What | Target | Measured |
|---|---|---|
| Space against a direct convolution (20,000-tap stereo response, 333-frame buffers) | within 1e-5 | worst error 1.9e-6 (output peak 7.1) |
| `PartitionedConvolver` against a direct convolution (50 … 5,000 taps, partitions 64 … 256, buffers 1 … 100) | within 1e-5 | passes |
| Linear Phase EQ impulse response (low cut, low shelf, a bell, high shelf) | symmetric about its latency | peak at 2,048 = `LatencySamples`; the two halves agree within 1.1e-7 (FFT rounding; the FIR itself is mirrored exactly); magnitude within 0.25 dB of the bands' RBJ curves from 150 Hz to 18 kHz |
| Linear Phase EQ flat | a pure delay | residual below −110 dB |
| Multipressor at rest | flat | ±0.05 dB from 30 Hz to 16 kHz |

Pitch Correction on a held tone with three harmonics, response 0, measured over the second second:

| Input | Key, scale | Target | Output | Error |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| A4 + 35 ¢ | C chromatic | A4 440 Hz | 439.98 Hz | −0.07 ¢ |
| A4 − 42 ¢ | C chromatic | A4 | 439.99 Hz | −0.05 ¢ |
| C4 + 25 ¢ | C chromatic | C4 261.63 Hz | 261.62 Hz | −0.05 ¢ |
| A♯4 + 30 ¢ | C major | B4 493.88 Hz | 493.86 Hz | −0.06 ¢ |
| G3 − 30 ¢ | G minor | G3 196 Hz | 196.00 Hz | −0.03 ¢ |
| D3 + 20 ¢ | D major | D3 146.83 Hz | 146.83 Hz | +0.02 ¢ |

The test's bound is 10 ¢. Detune shifts the target (a 20 ¢ flat A with +30 ¢ detune lands 20 … 40 ¢ sharp), and an
in-tune note keeps its pitch within 2 ¢ and its level within 1 dB. The Pitch Shifter lands within 5 ¢ of ±12, +7
semitones and +50 ¢ (by the waveform's period).

### Tempo sync

Against a supplied `TransportContext` (`Support/TransportRun`: a context per 64-frame block, positions integrated
block by block), with a tempo **step** (120 → 87 bpm) and a **ramp** (140 → 75 bpm over 2.9 s), each starting at a
song position that is not a grid line:
- Step FX volume steps change on the first sample at or after each sixteenth (every edge, to the sample).
- Beat Breaker slices start on their song positions (a muted slice is silent 30 samples in; the fades are 24 samples).
- Remix FX's gater edges: its 1 ms one-pole crosses half way 32 … 36 samples after each 32nd-note line (33.3 expected).
- Phat FX's LFO cutoff equals `cutoff · 2^(4·depth·sin(2π·quarter))` at every control point, to 1e-6.
- The Delay Designer's grid follows a tempo step (250 ms snaps to 2 sixteenths at 120 bpm, to 333.3 ms at 90 bpm).
- The Remix FX repeater captures the eighth begun before the touch and loops it (12,000 samples at 120 bpm, 14,400 at
  100 bpm).
- The T1 Stereo Delay, Tape Delay and Tremolo follow the transport's tempo over their `tempo` parameter.
- Step FX runs free at its `tempo` while stopped (an eighth at 100 bpm is 14,400 samples, exactly).
- Every unsynced effect (the other T2s, the Channel EQ, Compressor, Reverb) gives bit-identical output with and without
  a transport.

### Allocations

None in `Process`, `SetTransport`, `SetParameter` or `Reset` after `Prepare`, for every built-in: the contract theory
sweeps every parameter while processing; a second theory processes 300 blocks with a moving transport (tempo, position,
play/stop) at each effect's preset. `PartitionedConvolver.Tick` and `SetFilter` allocate nothing.

## For the other workstreams

### Engine (A)

- **Latency** (`LatencySamples`, reported at the current settings, for delay compensation): Linear Phase EQ
  `DesignSize / 2` (2,048 at 44.1/48 kHz, 4,096 at 88.2/96 kHz); Pitch Correction 2·⌈rate/80⌉ + 2 (1,202 at 48 kHz);
  Enveloper its lookahead (0 … 20 ms, a stepped parameter). Every other T2 is zero. (T1: the limiters, as before.)
- **`SetTransport`** matters to Step FX, Beat Breaker, Remix FX, Phat FX (tempo and song position) and the Delay
  Designer, Stereo Delay, Tape Delay, Tremolo and AutoFilter (tempo). Pass it before every `Process`, with the block's
  context; across a cycle jump a new context is enough (the effects take the position as given). The others ignore it.
  When a context is not renewed, an effect advances the last one by the frames it processed.
- The `tempo` parameter of those effects is ignored while a transport is passed, so the engine setting it from the
  context's bpm (as it now does) is harmless; it is what a stand-alone render uses.
- Contexts per 32-frame piece (automated inserts) are fine: an effect reads the position at each sample from the context
  of the `Process` call it is in. `SetTransport` is implemented on `AudioEffect`, the class that lists `IAudioEffect`,
  so calls through the interface reach it; a theory calls it through `IAudioEffect` for every synced built-in and checks
  that the output follows the transport.
- **IR loading.** `ConvolutionReverb` (`anode.space`) implements `IFileEffect` (new, `Anode.Dsp.Effects`):
  `FileTypes` (`.wav`), `File`, `LoadFile(path)` on the control thread while not processing. Its state carries the path
  (the instruments' `ANIS` blob, as the Sampler's), so `LoadState` reloads the file; it returns false (keeping the
  built-in hall) when the file is gone. The engine needs a way to call `LoadFile` on an insert, the counterpart of an
  instrument's `spec.File`.
- Space allocates its convolvers for the response it has at `Prepare`/`LoadFile` (a 10 s stereo response is about
  16 MB of spectra); the built-in hall is about 4 MB.

### Editor (C)

- New categories (display strings, as the T1 ones): `Pitch`, `Specialized`, `Multi Effects`. The insert menu ranks
  unknown categories after the known ones; add them to its order if wanted.
- **Delay Designer** taps: `tap{X}Time` (ms, 0 … 10,000), `tap{X}Level` (dB, −60 … 6), `tap{X}Pan` (−100 … 100),
  `tap{X}LowCut` (Hz, 20 = off), `tap{X}HighCut` (Hz, 20,000 = off), `tap{X}Mute` (toggle), X = `A` … `Z`; with
  `taps` (1 … 26: taps past it are inactive), `sync`, `grid` (note value), `feedbackTap` (A … Z), `feedback`, `mix`.
  Regex: `^tap([A-Z])(Time|Level|Pan|LowCut|HighCut|Mute)$`.
- **Multipressor** bands: `band{n}On`, `band{n}Threshold`, `band{n}Ratio`, `band{n}ExpThreshold`, `band{n}ExpRatio`,
  `band{n}Reduction`, `band{n}Attack`, `band{n}Release`, `band{n}Gain`, n = 1 … 4 (low to high); crossovers `cross1` …
  `cross3` (Hz; kept in order a third of an octave apart); `output`, `mix`. Regex:
  `^band([1-4])(On|Threshold|Ratio|ExpThreshold|ExpRatio|Reduction|Attack|Release|Gain)$`. The reading is the deepest
  band's gain change; each band's is `Multipressor.BandGain(band)` (not yet on the wire).
- **Step FX** steps: `cutoff{n}`, `volume{n}` (0 … 100 %), `pan{n}` (−100 … 100), n = 1 … 16.
- **Beat Breaker** slices: `slice{n}Source` (1 … 16), `slice{n}Reverse`, `slice{n}Mute`, n = 1 … 16.
- **Remix FX** pads: `xy1Active`, `xy1Effect` (Filter, Wobble, Orbit), `xy1X`, `xy1Y`; `xy2Active`, `xy2Effect`
  (Repeater, Reverse, Scratch, Gater, Downsampler, Tape Stop), `xy2X`, `xy2Y` (X and Y 0 … 1); `tempo`. A touch is
  `xy{n}Active` = 1, its release 0: the performance recording automates exactly these.
- The Linear Phase EQ uses the Channel EQ's parameter ids, so its editor can be the Channel EQ's.

## Contract notes

- `EffectCategories` gains `Pitch`, `Specialized` and `MultiEffects` (`"Multi Effects"`).
- `IFileEffect` (new interface) for effects that load files; only Space implements it.
- `AudioEffect.SetTransport` is a public method implementing the interface member (the default stays empty for other
  implementations); `EffectParameters.Describe` takes the macro hints as trailing indices (the `Macro(index, order)`
  contract method is what it calls).
- Space's class is `ConvolutionReverb` (namespace `Effects.Space`, which a class named `Space` would clash with); its id
  is `anode.space`.
